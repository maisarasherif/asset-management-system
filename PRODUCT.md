# Product

## Register

product

## Users

The system serves internal administrators and operators who manage assets, components, certificates, routine maintenance, catalog configuration, scheduler runs, and client access. It also serves read-only internal users and client users who need to inspect assigned project assets and certificate records without unnecessary operational controls.

## Product Purpose

AMS helps Porto Marine Services keep asset records, component structure, certificate status, expiry dates, competency data, and project access in one operational workspace. Success means users can identify certificate risk quickly, move from an asset to its components and certificates without losing context, and perform administrative changes with confidence.

## Brand Personality

Precise, operational, and calm. The interface should feel trustworthy for marine and industrial compliance work, with enough polish for executive review but without decorative dashboard theatrics.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic AWS-console blandness, marketing-style SaaS hero patterns, decorative cards, ambiguous icon-only controls, and heavy visual treatments that compete with task completion. Avoid making operational workflows feel like a sales dashboard.

## Design Principles

- Keep asset context visible while users move through related workflows.
- Make compliance risk scannable before asking users to inspect details.
- Prefer clear operational language over abstract product copy.
- Preserve Cloudscape familiarity where it helps users work faster.
- Use visual emphasis for state, priority, and current location, not decoration.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Aim for WCAG AA. The UI should remain usable with keyboard navigation, visible focus states, sufficient contrast, reduced motion preferences, and non-color status cues for certificate validity and operational risk.
